What Is a Good Typing Speed? WPM Explained

By TypingOcto Team·Typing speed·November 5, 2026·3 min read

You have taken a typing test and got a number. But is it good? Here is how typing speed is measured and what the common benchmarks look like.

What does WPM mean?

WPM stands for words per minute. Because words have different lengths, typing tests count every 5 characters (including spaces and punctuation) as one word. So if you type 200 characters in one minute, your speed is 40 WPM.

Most tests also show your accuracy: the percentage of keystrokes that were correct. A fast speed with poor accuracy is not very useful in real life, because you have to go back and fix the errors.

What is the average typing speed?

The figure most often quoted for the average adult is around 40 WPM. People who type a lot for work or study are often faster, while people who use two fingers ("hunt and peck") are usually slower.

What speed do you need for a job?

Requirements vary a lot between employers, but as a rough guide, many office and administrative roles ask for somewhere around 40–50 WPM, while data entry and transcription jobs often expect more. If a job lists a typing requirement, practise with tests of the same length (often 1 to 5 minutes).

Accuracy matters as much as speed

Try to keep your accuracy at 95% or higher. If you type 60 WPM with lots of mistakes, your real, useful speed is much lower once you fix them. On TypingOcto, lessons give you three stars only when you reach 96% accuracy.

💡 A good goal for most people is 50+ WPM with 95% accuracy. It makes everyday typing feel effortless.

Frequently asked questions

Is 40 WPM a good typing speed?

Yes. Around 40 WPM is commonly considered average, and it is enough for everyday tasks like emails and school work.

Is 60 WPM fast?

60 WPM is a fast typing speed. It is comfortably above average and suitable for most office jobs.

How is WPM calculated?

WPM counts every 5 typed characters, including spaces, as one word, then divides by the number of minutes.
