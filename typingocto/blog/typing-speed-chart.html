The typing speed chart

SpeedLevelNext step
Under 20 WPM🐣 BeginnerLearn the home row and stop looking at the keys.
20–30 WPM🌱 LearnerFinish the top and bottom row lessons.
30–40 WPM🙂 Getting therePractise common words and keep 95% accuracy.
40–50 WPM👍 AverageWork on capitals, numbers and punctuation.
50–60 WPM🚀 Above averageTrain your weak keys and longer texts.
60–80 WPM⚡ FastFocus on rhythm and reading ahead.
80–100 WPM🔥 Very fastShort speed bursts and endurance tests.
100+ WPM👑 ExpertYou could compete in typing races!

These ranges are a general guide, not an official standard. Different tests give slightly different results depending on the text and the duration.

Why your score changes between tests

  • Text difficulty: long or rare words, capitals and punctuation lower your WPM.
  • Test length: 15-second tests are usually faster than 2-minute tests.
  • Warm-up: most people type faster after a few minutes of practice.

To track real progress, use the same test settings each time. Our progress page draws your WPM and accuracy over time.

How to move up a level

From beginner to around 40 WPM, the most important thing is learning to touch type with the correct fingers. After that, improvements come from accuracy, rhythm and practising the keys that slow you down. Our 10 tips to type faster explain how.

💡 Improve by 5 WPM at a time. Small goals are easier to reach and keep you motivated.

Frequently asked questions

What typing speed is considered professional?

Speeds above 70–80 WPM with high accuracy are typical of people who type professionally all day.

Why is my typing speed different on every test?

Text difficulty, test length and how warmed up you are all affect your score. Use the same settings to compare results fairly.
