How Long Does It Take to Learn Touch Typing?

By TypingOcto Team·November 15, 2026·3 min read

It is the question everyone asks before they start. The honest answer: it depends on how often you practise, but you can feel real progress within a few weeks.

In this article
  1. The short answer
  2. A realistic week-by-week plan
  3. What affects how fast you learn
  4. The "slow phase" is normal
  5. How to stay motivated
  6. FAQ

The short answer

With around 15 minutes of practice per day, many people learn the full keyboard layout within 2 to 4 weeks, and then keep getting faster over the following months. If you practise only once a week, it takes much longer, because muscle memory needs repetition.

What affects how fast you learn

  • Consistency: daily practice is the biggest factor.
  • Old habits: if you type fast with two fingers, it can take a bit longer to switch.
  • Not looking down: peeking at the keys slows down learning.
  • Accuracy: practising mistakes teaches your fingers the wrong movements.

The "slow phase" is normal

When you switch to touch typing, you will probably be slower than before for one or two weeks. This is the moment many people give up. Keep going: once your fingers learn the positions, your speed will pass your old speed.

Frequently asked questions

Can I learn touch typing in a week?

You can learn the home row and part of the keyboard in a week, but most people need a few weeks of daily practice to type comfortably without looking.

How many hours does it take to learn typing?

Many learners get comfortable with the full keyboard after roughly 5 to 10 hours of focused practice spread over several weeks.
