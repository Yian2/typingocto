Free online typing lessons for beginners and pros

TypingOcto teaches touch typing step by step: start with the home row (A S D F J K L ;), then the top and bottom rows, capital letters, numbers and punctuation. Each of the 100 typing lessons shows a colour-coded keyboard so you always know which finger to use.

How fast should I type?

The average person types around 40 words per minute (WPM). With 10–15 minutes of daily practice most people reach 50–60 WPM. Take our free typing speed test to measure your WPM and accuracy, or read what a good typing speed is.

Why an octopus?

Inky has eight arms, and you have ten fingers. Every lesson or typing test earns coins you can spend on food, hats and colors for Inky. If you don't practise for a few days, Inky gets hungry, a friendly reminder to keep your streak going.
