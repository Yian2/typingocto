Home Row Keys Explained: Where to Put Your Fingers

By TypingOcto Team·Touch typing basics·November 9, 2026·3 min read

The home row is the middle row of letters on your keyboard, and it is the foundation of touch typing. Get this right and every other key becomes easier.

Which keys are the home row?

On a standard QWERTY keyboard, the home row keys are A S D F for the left hand and J K L ; for the right hand. The G and H keys sit in the middle and are reached by stretching your index fingers.

Why do F and J have bumps?

Look closely at your keyboard: the F and J keys have small raised lines or dots. They let your index fingers find the home position without looking. Whenever you feel lost, touch the bumps and you are back home.

Why the home row matters

Every movement in touch typing starts and ends at the home row. Your fingers reach up or down to a key, then come back. Because the distances are always the same, your hands learn them by memory, and that is what makes fast typing possible.

Correct hand position

  • Curve your fingers slightly, as if holding a small ball.
  • Rest your fingertips lightly on the keys; don't press them down.
  • Keep your wrists straight and slightly raised, not resting on the desk.
  • Keep your thumbs relaxed just above the space bar.

Common mistakes

  • Drifting hands: after a while your hands slide one key to the side. Check the bumps often.
  • Using the wrong finger for G or H: these belong to the index fingers.
  • Lifting the whole hand: only the finger that types should move.

💡 Try typing "a sad lad asks dad" using only the home row. If you can type it without looking, you are ready for the top row!

Frequently asked questions

What are the 8 home row keys?

A, S, D, F for the left hand and J, K, L, ; for the right hand.

Which fingers go on F and J?

Your left and right index fingers. The bumps on these keys help you find them without looking.
